After years of building things and meaning to write them up, I'm finally doing it.

This blog is where I'll document things I find interesting: CTF writeups, infrastructure experiments, notes on tools and techniques I keep reaching for. No particular schedule, no particular audience in mind — mostly a personal log that might be useful to someone else.

## What to expect

- **CTF writeups** — post-competition analysis of challenges I found interesting or frustrating.
- **Engineering notes** — setup guides, debugging sessions, architecture decisions worth capturing.
- **Short takes** — observations that don't fit anywhere else.

The site is built with Jekyll and served via Docker + Traefik. Source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/richmartins).

Let's go.
